Information We Collect
When you opt in to receive SMS communications from EMA Puget Sound, we may collect:
- Your mobile phone number
- Your name and contact information
- Appointment and service-related information
- Your messaging preferences
- Any information you provide through text communications with us

How We Use SMS Information
We use SMS messaging to:
- Communicate with patients regarding appointments and scheduling
- Respond to patient inquiries and service-related questions
- Provide care coordination and follow-up communications
- Send important practice-related notifications
- Conduct conversational business communications related to patient care and services

SMS Consent
By providing your mobile number and opting in, you consent to receive conversational and service-related SMS messages from EMA Puget Sound. Message frequency may vary depending on your interactions with our office.

Message and data rates may apply based on your mobile carrier plan.

Opting Out
You may opt out of SMS communications at any time by replying STOP to any text message. After opting out, you will no longer receive SMS messages from us unless you opt in again.

Information Sharing
EMA Puget Sound does not sell, rent, or share your mobile phone number or SMS consent information with third parties for marketing purposes.

We may share information with service providers who assist us in operating our messaging services, provided they maintain the confidentiality and security of your information and use it only for authorized business purposes.

Data Security
We implement reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to protect your personal information from unauthorized access, disclosure, or misuse.

Please note that SMS communications are not always secure, and you should avoid sending highly sensitive medical or personal information by text message unless specifically instructed by our office.

HIPAA Considerations
As a healthcare provider, EMA Puget Sound is committed to complying with applicable healthcare privacy laws, including the Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act (“HIPAA”), where applicable.
